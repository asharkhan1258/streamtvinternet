About Infinity support LLC

Infinity support LLC is an independent comparison and referral platform operated by Infinity support LLC . We help households compare internet, TV, phone, and bundle options available at their address, so they can choose the plan that fits their needs and budget without spending hours researching providers.

We are not a service provider. We do not own networks, sell service, or handle your billing. What we do is make the comparison simple: you tell us your location and what you’re looking for, and we help you review the options available to you and connect you with the provider you choose.

What We Do

  • Compare plans in one place. Instead of checking each provider separately, you can review available internet, TV, phone, and bundle options side by side.
  • Match options to your address. Availability varies by location, so we focus on what’s actually offered where you live.
  • Help you decide, not pressure you. Reviewing options through our platform is free and comes with no obligation. The choice is always yours.
  • Connect you to the provider. When you find a plan you like, we help refer you to that provider, who then handles the sign-up, installation, and service.

How We Make Money

We’re upfront about this: we earn a commission from providers when you sign up for a service through our referral. This is how we keep the comparison service free for you. Because these commercial relationships exist, they may influence which providers or plans appear on our platform. All pricing, contract terms, installation, billing, and customer support are provided by the provider you select — not by Stream TV Internet.
